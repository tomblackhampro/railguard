Our first client project

RailGuard

AI & IoT prototype concept

Detect before damage.

RailGuard explores a system for detecting potential vandalism around parked trains. Cameras and sensors would monitor a protected zone, with onboard Linux processing designed to identify people, filter irrelevant movement and generate alerts.

Potential future improvements include a mobile app to send incident details to headquarters and machine learning to reduce false alarms.

Privacy by design: Video analysis should happen locally where possible, with limited data sharing, appropriate safeguards and GDPR considerations built into the design.

RailGuard is a client project being developed by Yantra. The system is a student prototype concept, not an operational security product.
