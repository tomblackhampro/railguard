Smart IoT railway protection

Detect before damage.

RailGuard explores a smarter way to protect railway carriages. Our team is designing a connected monitoring concept that identifies suspicious activity early and helps security teams respond.

Explore our concept
What we're building

Early detection. Clearer decisions.

Our concept brings sensors, local processing and a connected dashboard together to support railway security. It is currently a student engineering project under development.

01

Sense

Monitor a protected zone around railway carriages.

02

Process

Evaluate sensor information locally, including distinguishing people from small animals.

03

Connect

Send relevant events and system status to an IoT platform.

04

Alert

Present useful information for security and operational teams.
